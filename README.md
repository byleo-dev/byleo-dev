# Hi, I’m Le0 👋

**Founder of LEO DIGITAL STUDIO | AI Automation & Workflow Builder**

I design and build AI workflows for two stages of client work: reviewing incoming requests and organizing the information and steps needed to start a new client project.

## What I build

### 📥 AI Intake & Triage

I build workflows that check incoming requests for missing information and duplicates, use AI to summarize and classify them, and route them for review. The reviewer decides what happens next and how to respond to the client.

**Best fit for:** Businesses that handle a substantial volume of written requests. When requests are incomplete, unclear, lengthy, duplicated, or urgent, the workflow helps reviewers see what needs attention first.

### 📋 AI Client Onboarding

I build workflows that turn new-client information into a structured client record, a project summary, and an onboarding checklist. The person responsible reviews the information and decides when work can begin.

**Best fit for:** Digital and marketing agencies, software and design studios, consulting and professional services firms, freelance teams, and B2B service companies. It also fits teams that collect client information through forms or email and need someone to review it before work begins.
